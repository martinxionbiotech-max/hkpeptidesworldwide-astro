The following uses are expressly prohibited, and purchasing from us constitutes your agreement not to engage in them:

We do not provide — and will not provide — information for human use. That includes:

Storage, reconstitution-for-laboratory-use and stability information published on this site is provided strictly for laboratory handling of research material by qualified personnel.
