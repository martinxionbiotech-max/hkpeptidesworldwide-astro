## Research Background

PT-141 is a cyclic heptapeptide melanocortin receptor agonist selective for MC3R and MC4R. Unlike Melanotan-II (which activates MC1R causing pigmentation), PT-141 was engineered for CNS melanocortin receptor selectivity. Research applications focus on central melanocortin pathways regulating sexual arousal independent of dopaminergic and serotonergic systems.

The 10 mg x 10 vials configuration is manufactured under strict quality control with HPLC, MS, and AAA verification. Batch-specific COA included. Bulk and OEM configurations available.

## Molecular Mechanisms

### MC3R/MC4R Activation

Selective agonism at central melanocortin receptors in hypothalamic and limbic regions modulating sexual arousal pathways.

### Non-Dopaminergic Mechanism

PT-141 activates melanocortin pathways distinct from dopaminergic agents providing a tool for studying melanocortin-mediated sexual behavior.

### Cyclic Peptide Stability

The Asp2-Lys7 lactam bridge creates a constrained cyclic conformation enhancing metabolic stability and receptor selectivity.

## Comparative Analysis

Compared to Melanotan-II: PT-141 has greater MC4R selectivity and reduced MC1R activation reducing pigmentation effects. Both are cyclic melanocortin analogues.
