## Molecular Mechanisms

### BPC-157: VEGF-Driven Angiogenesis

Upregulates VEGF at injury sites → new capillary formation → enhanced tissue perfusion. Creates the vascular infrastructure for tissue repair.

### TB-500: Actin-Mediated Cell Migration

G-actin sequestration → maintains polymerization-ready actin pool → lamellipodia/filopodia formation → directional cell migration into wound beds. Mobilizes the cellular repair machinery.

### Synergistic Pathway Integration

BPC-157's NO-mediated vasodilation enhances delivery of TB-500-mobilized repair cells to injury sites. Simultaneous VEGF upregulation + actin dynamics activation produces greater net tissue repair than either peptide alone in preclinical wound models.
