## Research Background

GHRP-6 is the prototypical ghrelin receptor agonist and the most orexigenic GHRP peptide. Its hexapeptide sequence closely mimics the N-terminal active core of ghrelin, producing potent activation of both GH-releasing and appetite-stimulating GHS-R1a signaling pathways. ## Molecular Mechanisms

### GHS-R1a Agonism

High-affinity ghrelin receptor binding → dual signaling: (1) pituitary GH release via PLC/Ca²⁺, (2) hypothalamic NPY/AgRP neuron activation → hunger signaling.

### NPY/AgRP Activation

GHS-R1a on arcuate nucleus NPY/AgRP neurons → the most potent orexigenic circuit in the mammalian brain. GHRP-6 activates this pathway more strongly than GHRP-2 or Ipamorelin.

### GI Motility

Ghrelin-mimetic activity on enteric neurons → gastric emptying and intestinal transit via vagal afferent activation.

**Q: Why does GHRP-6 increase hunger?**
