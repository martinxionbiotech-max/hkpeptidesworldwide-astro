## Molecular Mechanisms

### GHRH Signal (CJC-1295)

GHRH-R activation on pituitary somatotrophs → cAMP/PKA → GH synthesis and vesicle mobilization. The no-DAC variant produces ~30 minute GHRH-R activation → discrete, physiological GH pulse.

### Somatostatin Suppression (Ipamorelin)

Selective GHS-R1a activation → reduced hypothalamic somatostatin tone → removal of tonic GH inhibition. Ipamorelin's selectivity minimizes ACTH/cortisol and hunger off-target effects.

### Synergistic GH Pulse

GHRH signal + somatostatin suppression → amplified GH release. GHRH opens the gate; GHS-R removes the brake. The resulting GH pulse is larger than either peptide alone and closely mimics endogenous pulsatile secretion.
