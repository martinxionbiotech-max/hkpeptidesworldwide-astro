## Research Background

Insulin-like Growth Factor-1 (IGF-1) is a 70-amino acid polypeptide that serves as the primary mediator of Growth Hormone's anabolic effects. Produced primarily in the liver in response to GH-induced GHR-JAK2-STAT5 signaling, IGF-1 circulates in a ternary complex with IGFBP-3 and ALS (acid-labile subunit). In research applications, recombinant IGF-1 enables direct study of IGF-1R signaling independent of GH receptor activation — allowing researchers to dissect GH's IGF-1-dependent effects from its direct GHR-mediated actions. IGF-1 research spans skeletal muscle hypertrophy, bone growth, neuroprotection, and cellular senescence pathways.

## Molecular Mechanisms

### IGF-1R Activation

Binding to IGF-1 receptor (tyrosine kinase) → receptor autophosphorylation → IRS-1/PI3K/Akt and Shc/Ras/MAPK signaling cascades → protein synthesis, cell survival, and proliferation. IGF-1R shares structural homology with insulin receptor but has distinct ligand affinity and tissue distribution.

### PI3K/Akt/mTOR Pathway

IGF-1R → IRS-1 → PI3K → PIP₃ → Akt → mTORC1 activation → protein translation initiation (via 4E-BP1 and S6K1). This is the primary anabolic signaling cascade mediating IGF-1's effects on skeletal muscle protein synthesis and hypertrophy.

### Cell Survival (Anti-Apoptotic)

Akt-mediated phosphorylation and inactivation of Bad, caspase-9, and FOXO transcription factors → mitochondrial stabilization and reduced apoptosis. Important in neuroprotection and cardioprotection research.

### IGFBP Regulation

IGF-1 bioavailability is tightly regulated by six IGF-binding proteins (IGFBP-1 through -6). IGFBP-3 is the major circulating carrier, forming a ternary complex with ALS that extends IGF-1 half-life from minutes to ~15 hours.

## Comparative Analysis

Compared to HGH: IGF-1 bypasses the GH receptor and directly activates IGF-1R. HGH activates the full GHR cascade including both IGF-1-dependent and direct GH effects. IGF-1 is preferred when studying isolated IGF-1R signaling; HGH is preferred when studying the complete GH response. Compared to PEG-MGF: PEG-MGF is the IGF-1Ec splice variant that specifically targets muscle satellite cells. Standard IGF-1 has broader tissue distribution and receptor activation profile.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How does IGF-1 differ from HGH?**

HGH activates the GH receptor → multiple downstream effects including IGF-1 production. IGF-1 directly activates the IGF-1 receptor → downstream anabolic signaling. HGH = indirect (via IGF-1); IGF-1 = direct receptor activation. Research choice depends on whether studying the complete GH axis (HGH) or isolated IGF-1R signaling (IGF-1).

**Q: What is the IGF-1/IGFBP-3/ALS ternary complex?**

In circulation, >99% of IGF-1 is bound to IGFBP-3 and ALS in a 150 kDa ternary complex. This extends IGF-1 half-life from minutes (free) to ~15 hours (bound) and prevents hypoglycemia from insulin receptor cross-activation. Research-grade IGF-1 is supplied as free peptide for controlled reconstitution.

**Q: What distinguishes IGF-1 from PEG-MGF?**

PEG-MGF is the IGF-1Ec splice variant with a 24-aa E-domain that specifically targets muscle satellite (stem) cells. Standard IGF-1 (systemic/liver-derived) has broader tissue activity. PEG-MGF = satellite cell-specific; IGF-1 = systemic anabolic signaling.
