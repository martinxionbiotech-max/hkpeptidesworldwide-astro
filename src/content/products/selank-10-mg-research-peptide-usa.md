For laboratory research applications, several pharmacokinetic parameters inform experimental design:

- **Reconstitution:** Lyophilized SELANK should be reconstituted in sterile, preservative-free aqueous buffer (PBS pH 7.4 or sterile water) to the desired stock concentration. Gentle swirling — not vortexing — is recommended to prevent peptide aggregation and ensure complete dissolution.

For extended storage, aliquot into single-use volumes and store at -20°C or -80°C.
