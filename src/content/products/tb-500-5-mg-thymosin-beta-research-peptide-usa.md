## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How does TB-500 differ from Thymosin Beta-4?**

TB-500 is the 7-aa actin-binding domain (LKKTETQ). Full TB4 is 43 aa including additional regulatory regions. TB-500 is preferred for actin-specific studies; TB4 for full biological context.

**Q: What is G-actin sequestration?**

TB-500 binds monomeric G-actin 1:1, preventing spontaneous polymerization while maintaining the actin pool ready for rapid filament assembly when needed. This actin-buffering is critical for cell motility.

**Q: How should TB-500 be stored?**
