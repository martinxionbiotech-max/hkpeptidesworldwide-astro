For laboratory research applications, several pharmacokinetic parameters inform experimental design:

- **Reconstitution:** Lyophilized SNAP-8 should be reconstituted in sterile, preservative-free aqueous buffer (PBS pH 7.4 or sterile water) to the desired stock concentration. Gentle swirling — not vortexing — is recommended to prevent peptide aggregation and ensure complete dissolution.

For extended storage, aliquot into single-use volumes and store at -20°C or -80°C. Preliminary dose-ranging experiments (logarithmic dilution series: 0.1, 1, 10, 100, 1000 nM and 1, 10, 100 μM) are recommended to establish the optimal concentration range for each specific research application.
