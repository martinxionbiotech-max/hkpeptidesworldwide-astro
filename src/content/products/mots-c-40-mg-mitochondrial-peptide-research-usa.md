For laboratory research applications, several pharmacokinetic parameters inform experimental design:

- **Reconstitution:** Lyophilized MOTS-C should be reconstituted in sterile, preservative-free aqueous buffer (PBS pH 7.4 or sterile water) to the desired stock concentration. Gentle swirling — not vortexing — is recommended to prevent peptide aggregation and ensure complete dissolution.
